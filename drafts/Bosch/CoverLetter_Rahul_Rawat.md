Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Master Thesis on Graph Based Question Answering and Retrieval Augmented Generation Systems at Robert Bosch GmbH. For the past year I have been building a modular Retrieval Augmented Generation orchestrator around Llama 3.1 8b served through Groq. I designed a router that classifies user intent into local vector retrieval from a ChromaDB store, external DuckDuckGo search, or direct conversational logic, and layered a stateful memory agent on top so context survives across turns. That work gives me a very concrete starting point for exploring how symbolic reasoning over RDF and OWL knowledge graphs, combined with SPARQL retrieval, can improve faithfulness and answer accuracy compared to purely embedding based baselines.
